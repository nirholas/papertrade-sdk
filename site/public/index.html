Verified against mainnet

  • Signatures. A real batched Close from HyperEVM recovers the trader's session key, and the SDK reproduces the relayer's intent ids.
  • Staking domain. The PaperStaking EIP-712 domain hashes to the contract's on-chain separator.
  • Liquidation prices. Exact on 205 of 205 sampled live positions.
  • Closes. Reproduces 15 of 15 leaderboard wins, a losing close's PAPER basis, and a mainnet liquidation.
  • Staking rewards. Matches PaperStaking.pendingReward to the wei.
